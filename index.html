01 · Research

Research

My research lies at the intersection of algebraic combinatorics and topology. I am particularly interested in matroids, hyperplane arrangements, and algebraic structures that encode their topology.

Dissertation

The Orlik–Solomon Algebra for Locally Geometric Posets

My dissertation, advised by Christin Bibby, develops a generalization of the Orlik–Solomon algebra to locally geometric posets and studies its connections with the topology of abelian arrangements.

Research interests

  • Algebraic combinatorics
  • Algebraic topology
  • Computational algebra
  • Matroids
  • Hyperplane and abelian arrangements
